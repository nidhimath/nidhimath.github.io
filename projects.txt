4. Camera Trajectory Analysis for Video Generation

summary: This project investigates whether training data rarity explains geometric failures in pose-conditioned video diffusion models (DFoT) by generating 3D point clouds and Gaussian Splats from RealEstate10K bedroom scenes under out-of-distribution camera trajectories. Depth Anything V2 was used to estimate per-pixel metric depth, which was back-projected into dense point clouds and evaluated using Chamfer Distance, Hausdorff Distance, F-score, and ICP fitness across structured trajectory perturbations (oscillating roll, diagonal translation, rising spiral, extreme downward tilt). Results challenged the rarity hypothesis: reconstruction quality was non-monotonic with tilt angle, equally rare motions (roll vs. tilt) produced substantially different outcomes, and compound motions similar to training patterns (arc, crane) performed well regardless of individual  primitive frequency. The findings suggest model robustness depends more on semantic content and motion combinations than the raw frequency of individual camera primitives in training data.

date: Jan - May 2026
live link: https://nidhimath.github.io/camera-trajectory/
